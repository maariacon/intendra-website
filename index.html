The problem

You don't need more reports. You need a system that tells you what to do, who owns it, and what it's worth.

Most CX programs give you volume reports, sample QA audits, adherence scores, and post-contact surveys. None of them answer the only questions that matter: what should we do, who owns it, and what is it worth? So friction compounds, decisions stall, and customers leave before you ever find out why.

Too much data. Too little relevance.

Captured in the front, owned in the back

The problem surfaces in one place. The fix lives in another.

Most of what customers contact you about — and much of what they never say — starts somewhere else: a refund policy, a checkout step, a missed SLA. The teams closest to the customer absorb the cost; the fix belongs to product, logistics, or billing. Intendra reads it all, spoken and silent, and assigns each intent to its owner, with its cost and cause.
