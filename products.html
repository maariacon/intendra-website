Why did customers contact us, and what did it cost?

Pulse™

Every interaction classified against a proprietary intent library, with cost and effort attached. 100% coverage, >97% accuracy.

Example

49 agents, 8,500 contacts a week, FCR stuck at 68%. Pulse surfaced $11K a week in contacts that shouldn't exist — most of them digitisable.

What it does

Replaces

Disposition codes nobody trusts, 1–3% QA sampling, single-digit survey response.

Does

  • Intent on every interaction — a verb: cancel, refund, expedite
  • The trigger behind it, and its handling and downstream cost
  • Customer effort, per interaction
  • 100% QA scoring — no sampling

Hands off to Compass ↓

What would make it stop, and who owns that?

Compass™

Root cause attributed, the action priced, and the owner assigned — the function that controls the lever.

Example

Compass traced 61% of billing disputes to one delayed-refund policy — roughly $553K a year, owned by Billing.

What it does

Replaces

The quarterly deep-dive, and prioritisation by volume or by whoever argues loudest.

Does

  • Root cause: people, process, policy, or external
  • Value-Irritant placement, to the customer and the company
  • Prescribed action — eliminate, digitise, simplify, leverage, preempt — priced
  • Owner assigned, with a baseline and a target

This is where Intendra stops. The initiative goes to the owner, in their tools.

Who is leaving without telling us?

Reveal™

92% of unhappy customers never complain. Reveal finds them before they go.

92%

never complain. Voice of Customer hears the other 8%. Voice of Data reads them all.

What it does

Replaces

Churn as a lagging indicator, and retention lists built only from complainers.

Does

  • Silent-signal detection across low-contact customers
  • Risk per account, ranked by probability and revenue
  • Cause linkage, learned from the customers who did speak up
  • Early enough to act
Only at Intendra

Who should handle what?

FIT

The best channel for each intent, and the best associate for each intent and customer type.

Example

WhatsApp resolved 82% first-contact against a lower rate on voice for the same intents. Fit surfaces which channel each intent belongs in.

What it does

Replaces

Routing by availability. "Whoever's free gets the call."

Does

  • Channel to intent — which channel resolves each best
  • Intent to associate — who performs best on which contact types
  • Customer to associate — first-time, high-value, at-risk, matched
  • Predicted vs. actual — you watch the prediction hold

Who do we coach, and on what?

Profile™

Every associate placed on effectiveness and efficiency, from 100% of their work.

Effectiveness → Efficiency →
Sleepers Effective, not efficient
Stars Effective and efficient
Weak links Neither
Anchors Efficient, not effective
What it does

Replaces

Adherence scores, handle-time league tables, and QA on three sampled calls.

Does

  • Effectiveness — FCR, quality, snowballs, customer effort
  • Efficiency — handle-time variance, transfers, long/short calls
  • Coaching intelligence drawn from real interactions
  • Scores that hold up when a partner disputes them
