Our Story

The same methodology. Months to days.

For two decades, the CX industry got better at listening and no better at acting. More surveys, more dashboards, more sentiment scores — and the same friction, the same cost, year after year.

We built Intendra on a different premise: customer contact is demand, and most of it can be eliminated. The methodology for finding, pricing, and removing it already existed, proven across 155+ enterprise engagements. It just couldn't scale past a consulting project — two months, four consultants, 10% of the data.

AI changed that. What took months now runs in days, at cents per interaction, across 100% of your customers.

"The best customer service is when customers don't need to contact you at all. Intendra exists to make that possible — systematically, at scale."

Bill Price — CEO & Co-founder
