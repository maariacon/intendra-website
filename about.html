Why we exist

Knowing Doing

The CX industry kept getting better at listening, and no better at acting. More surveys, more dashboards, more sentiment scores — and the same friction, the same cost, year after year. Intendra is built to end that: to turn what customers do into what your business should do about it.

Twenty-four years of method,
